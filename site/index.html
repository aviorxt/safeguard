GOOD FILTERS. HONEST LIMITS.

A useful layer.
Not a promise of invincibility.

DNS filtering works with domain names. It cannot inspect encrypted page paths, remove ads served from the same domain as a video, or enforce Safe Search by itself. Keep your resolver’s DNSSEC, Safe Search and other security settings enabled separately.

Something blocked by mistake? Tell us the domain and profile. We review requests before changing the lists.
